4. **Essential — BH needs valid p-values and a dependence check; Storey π₀ is not a binary count.** BH is justified under independence and certain positive-dependence conditions, not arbitrary dependence. ## Strongest argument against my recommendation

The IUT may have almost no power precisely where the user wants detections: if H_QP allows nearly coherent periodic phase variation, the max p-value will be controlled by that alternative. A hierarchical Bayesian mixture can pool weak evidence across stars and estimate f_bin more efficiently. But when the individual timing likelihoods overlap, that gain comes from population assumptions and priors; it does not establish that a particular star is binary.

## What I would need to check to be sure

I would first run the circular-LTTE versus long-coherence-H_QP identifiability experiment, then calibrate each null through the complete light-curve pipeline and selection rule. I would also verify common-mode uncertainty and joint delay/amplitude/coherence errors, benchmark bootstrap cost and tail resolution, and test f_bin recovery under alternative nuisance mixtures.
