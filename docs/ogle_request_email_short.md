**Subject:** Request: post-2016 OGLE-IV light curves of LMC RR Lyrae

Dear Igor,

In recent work we have argued that a sizeable fraction of RR Lyrae, in particular the metal-rich ones, may form through
binary evolution (Iorio & Belokurov 2021; Bobrick et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2025; Iorio et al. 2026). We would now like
to test this directly by constraining the binary properties of RR Lyrae through the light-travel-time effect in their
O−C diagrams. The LMC sample from OCVS, combined with MACHO, is ideal for this, but the public light curves end in 2016,
and the expected orbital periods (several years to decades) need a longer baseline.

Would the OGLE team be willing to share the OGLE-IV I-band (and V, if convenient) photometry obtained after 2016 for the
LMC RR Lyrae from the OCVS collection? Having the whole sample, rather than a pre-selected subset, would let us
re-run the full analysis with properly calibrated false-alarm rates. We would of course use the data under whatever
conditions you prefer, and we would be very happy to involve you and any interested members of the team.

Many thanks for considering this.

Best wishes,
Vasily Belokurov
Institute of Astronomy, University of Cambridge
